# Country clips: slugs, and my cautious suggestions

Each African country gets one clip of about 5 to 10 seconds, picked by you (or a person from that country), when a delegate chooses it on the first question. Non-African countries are not mentioned to the delegate; they quietly get `ghana.mp3`.

**I have deliberately not picked 54 songs.** A wrong 'most popular song' in front of that country's delegates is worse than a blank, and I cannot verify popularity from here. The only ones I am reasonably sure of are marked. Everything else: ask a person from that country, or the organisers.

Rights: each clip is a copyrighted recording. Get permission before hosting it. Crediting the song is good practice, not a licence.
